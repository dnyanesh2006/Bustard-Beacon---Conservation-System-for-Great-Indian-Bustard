Tourism & Visitor Guidelines

Great Indian Bustard Sanctuary, Nannaj

Important Notice

The Great Indian Bustard Sanctuary is primarily a conservation zone. To protect the critically endangered Bustard population and their natural habitat, all visits are strictly regulated.

  • Prior permission is mandatory for visiting core areas.
  • Do not litter, play loud music, or disturb the wildlife.
  • Vehicles must be parked at designated parking areas.
  • Follow the instructions of Forest Officers and Rangers at all times.
